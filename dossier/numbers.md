## 6. Cost of the AI, measured then projected

- One offer = 1 call (the model reads the club inventory and picks the slots), 1560 input tokens, 780 output tokens **[M]**.
- The realistic photos are generated once (7 images, about $0.47 in total). Placing the logo is then done by code, with no cost and no network call.
- Small model (claude-haiku-5.5, $0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens): **$0.00055** per offer; measured writing time: 3.5 to 4.5 s.
- Large model (claude-sonnet-5.5, $2 / $10 per million tokens): $0.01092 per offer.
- At the scale of the target: 7,800 offers a month, which is **$4.3 a month** with the small model (0.04% of monthly revenue), $85 with the large model (0.7%).
- So the cost of the AI is not the issue: hosting and support are bigger. The measured writing time (3.5 to 4.5 s) is above the 3 s target of the course; idea: show the text as it is written.
