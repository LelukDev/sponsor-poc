1 · Problem

Amateur clubs have space to sell and no time to sell it

Julien is the volunteer treasurer of a 320-member football club. Local sponsors bring about 9% of a budget of roughly €80,000. He writes each pitch alone, in the evening.

  • Generic pitches. The same template goes to a bakery and a garage.
  • Offers that cannot be sold. The jersey chest is already taken; a competitor of a current sponsor gets the same email.
  • No link to what the club needs. The minibus must be replaced, but nobody asks the garage.

Julien is an illustrative persona. The three pains are hypotheses we will test with 10 club calls.

2 · Solution

Type a business name, get an offer that fits the club, with the logo already on the kit

For volunteer-run clubs that need local sponsors, Sponsio is an AI sponsorship assistant that turns any business name into a ready-to-send offer in seconds. Unlike proposal templates, it offers only slots that are actually free, avoids conflicts with current sponsors and ties each offer to the club's needs.
